— ANSWERS & CLARITY

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers about hiring an SEO consultant for your Islamabad business.

Do you provide SEO services to businesses in Islamabad? +
Yes. I am based in Lahore and work with Islamabad businesses remotely through video calls, messaging and shared reports. Most SEO work happens in your website, Google Search Console and your Google Business Profile, so it does not depend on being in the same city.
How long does SEO take to show results? +
It depends on your competition, the current state of your website, and how much needs fixing or creating. Technical fixes can be picked up by Google sooner, while rankings and inquiries for competitive searches usually build over several months. I will give you a realistic view after the audit rather than a fixed date.
What is included in SEO services? +
The scope depends on your goals and website. It typically covers an SEO audit, competitor review, keyword-to-page mapping, technical fixes, on-page improvements, local SEO, content recommendations and regular reporting. We agree the exact scope after the audit.
Can you help my business rank on Google Maps? +
If your business is eligible for a Google Business Profile, yes. That means a real business that deals with customers in person, either at a location or within a defined service area. I work on profile accuracy, categories, services, local landing pages and consistent business details. I do not help with fake addresses or virtual offices, because they break Google's guidelines and put your profile at risk.
Do you guarantee first-page rankings? +
No. Nobody outside Google controls rankings, so any guarantee is a warning sign. What I can commit to is a clear plan, careful implementation, honest reporting and priorities based on what is likely to bring you relevant inquiries.
How much do SEO services in Islamabad cost? +
Cost depends on the size of your website, the competitiveness of your market and the scope of work. I do not publish a fixed price list. After the free audit, I send a proposal that shows what is included and what it costs, so you can decide with full information.
