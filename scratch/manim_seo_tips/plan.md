# Manim Video Production Plan: 10-Second Best SEO Tips

## Video Overview
- **Topic**: Best Tips for SEO (Search Engine Optimization)
- **Target Platform**: Short / Reel / Video Explainer (10 Seconds total)
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (Landscape) or 1080x1920 (Vertical Short) - 60 FPS
- **Color Palette**: Dark Slate background (`#0F172A`), Emerald Green (`#059669`), Gold Accent (`#F59E0B`), Cyan Blue (`#38BDF8`), Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`).

## Technical Constraints & Design Principles
- Monospace typography (`Courier` / `Consolas` / `Menlo` fallback) for crisp non-distorted Manim rendering.
- High opacity contrast (Primary elements 1.0, cards 0.8, background 0.1).
- Smooth entry transformations (`Write`, `Create`, `ReplacementTransform`, `FadeIn`).
